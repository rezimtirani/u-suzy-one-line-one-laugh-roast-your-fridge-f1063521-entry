One line roasting my own fridge: it has an ice maker, a water dispenser, and a touchscreen — and still can't keep a lettuce alive longer than my will to cook.

> My fridge has an ice maker, a water dispenser, and a touchscreen -- and still can't keep a lettuce alive longer than my will to cook.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the roast to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 200 characters of UTF-8 text, a single line.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
